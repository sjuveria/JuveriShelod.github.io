Hi! I am Juveria.

I come from a computer science background with real-world experience as a software developer, where I’ve built individual projects across multiple languages and ecosystems including Java, .NET, C#, Python, PHP, JavaScript, SQL, and iOS development. Over time, I’ve realized I’m most excited when I’m solving problems, understanding how systems work under the hood, and turning complexity into something simple and useful.
That curiosity has naturally led me to pivot into data analysis, where I see myself “playing with data” (professionally, of course) and turning raw numbers into meaningful insights that actually help people make decisions. Along the way, I’ve built hands-on projects using SQL, Excel, and Tableau, learning not just tools but how to think in data.
Outside of tech, I’ve worked as a customer representative, which taught me how real people think, how relationships are built, and why communication matters just as much as code. I’m comfortable presenting ideas, writing reports, and explaining things clearly—even before AI started helping everyone sound smart.
Beyond work, I enjoy nature, hiking, running, working out, and exploring new places—basically anything that reminds me the world is bigger than a bug in production.
In short: I like building things, breaking things (intentionally, mostly), understanding them deeply, and then turning that understanding into something useful.
